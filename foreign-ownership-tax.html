Legal Framework & Tax Compliance

Foreign Ownership & Tax Architecture

A transparent, ring-fenced legal structure designed for international investors: 100% foreign equity, zero worldwide tax exposure, and seamless legal profit repatriation.

Ring-Fenced Vietnam-Source Taxation

TERRITORIAL RING-FENCE

As foreign investors, your non-Vietnam assets and global incomes are strictly isolated. Taxation applies solely to the revenue and net profits generated by the Vietnamese operating entity in Ho Chi Minh City. There is zero global reporting or worldwide tax liability triggered by investing in Yuki-Chi.

100%

Foreign Ownership Allowed: Full WTO & Vietnam Law compliance for F&B restaurants and dessert cafes (no mandatory local nominee required).

20%

Corporate Income Tax (CIT): Fixed 20% on audited net profit generated exclusively from Vietnam store operations.

0%

Dividend Withholding Tax: 0% WHT on corporate profit dividends remitted to foreign corporate entities (5% for individual foreign investors).

Entity Setup & Capital Flow

100% Foreign-Invested Enterprise (FIE LLC)

1. Corporate Entity Structure

  • ✓
    Entity Type: Single-Member or Multi-Member Limited Liability Company (100% Foreign Owned LLC / Công ty TNHH).
  • ✓
    Direct Foreign Licensing: Holds an Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) issued by DPI HCMC.
  • ✓
    Full Equity Title: Investor holds 100% direct voting equity, clean cap table, and full asset rights over machinery, IP, and leases.

2. DICA Capital Account & Repatriation

  • ★
    Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA): All charter capital and investor funds are wired directly into a regulated tier-1 commercial bank (HSBC, Standard Chartered, or Vietcombank).
  • ★
    State Bank of Vietnam Compliance: Capital enters via DICA, ensuring automatic legal legitimacy for future capital gains & dividend remittances.
  • ★
    Annual Profit Remittance: Following annual audited accounts and standard 7-day tax notice, 100% of after-tax profits are wired freely to investor offshore bank accounts in USD/SGD.

Standard Operating Procedure

Annual Profit Distribution & Remittance Flow

📑

Step 1: Annual Statutory Audit

Annual financial audit conducted by an independent certified audit firm (such as Baker Tilly, Mazars, or Crowe) alongside the Corporate Income Tax (CIT) annual finalization.

🏛️

Step 2: 7-Day Tax Authority Notice

Submit formal notification of profit remittance to the HCMC Tax Department at least 7 working days prior to the transfer (Circular No. 186/2010/TT-BTC compliance).

💳

Step 3: Direct DICA Wire Transfer

The custodian commercial bank (HSBC/Vietcombank) processes the wire transfer from the DICA capital account directly to investor offshore accounts in USD, SGD, or JPY.
