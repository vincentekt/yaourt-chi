Structuring Options & Tax Ring-Fencing

Foreign Ownership & Investment Routes

Compare the 3 primary legal investment vehicles for foreign capital in Vietnam: Singapore/Overseas SPV, Direct 100% FIE LLC, or Local Nominee Trust.

Ring-Fenced Vietnam-Source Taxation

TERRITORIAL RING-FENCE

Under Vietnamese law, foreign investors' non-Vietnam assets and global incomes are strictly isolated. Taxation applies solely to the revenue and net profits generated by the Vietnamese operating entity in Ho Chi Minh City. There is zero worldwide tax reporting or global tax liability triggered by investing in Yuki-Chi.

100%

Foreign Equity Permitted: Full WTO compliance for F&B restaurants and dessert cafes (no mandatory local partner required).

20%

Corporate Income Tax (CIT): Fixed 20% on audited net profit generated exclusively in Vietnam.

0%

Dividend Withholding Tax: 0% WHT on corporate profit dividends remitted to foreign holding companies.

🛡️

Global Income Protection: Does a Foreign Stakeholder Pay Vietnam Tax on Overseas Income?

Answer: ABSOLUTELY NO. Your worldwide earnings outside Vietnam are 100% exempt from Vietnamese taxation.

1. Non-Resident Tax Status (Presence < 183 Days)

As a foreign investor who does not reside in Vietnam for more than 183 days per year, the General Department of Taxation classifies you as a Non-Resident. Under Vietnamese Personal Income Tax Law, non-residents are taxable strictly on Vietnam-sourced income only.

2. Complete Isolation of Overseas Wealth

Your salaries, corporate dividends, capital gains, properties, and stock portfolios in Singapore, US, UK, Hong Kong, or elsewhere do not need to be declared, filed, or taxed in Vietnam.

💡 Summary of What You Pay: The Vietnamese business pays 20% Corporate Income Tax (CIT) on its store profits in Saigon. The remaining after-tax profits are distributed to foreign stakeholders (0% WHT for corporate SPVs, or 5% PIT for individual dividend payouts). No additional tax or reporting on your global income is ever triggered.
Structuring Options

3 Alternative Ownership & Investment Pathways

Tailored structures balancing speed-to-market, legal asset protection, and tax efficiency.

OPTION A (RECOMMENDED) ★ Institutional

Singapore / Hong Kong Holding SPV

Investors pool funds into a clean Singapore (Pte. Ltd.) or HK holding entity, which in turn owns 100% of the Vietnam operating LLC.

  • ✓ 0% Dividend Withholding: Dividends flow out of Vietnam at 0% WHT under the Vietnam-Singapore DTA.
  • ✓ English Common Law Governance: Clean shareholders' agreement (SHA) governed by Singapore law & SIAC arbitration.
  • ✓ Easy Secondary Exits: Future equity sales or new investor rounds occur at the offshore SPV level without touching Vietnamese bureaucratic approvals.
Setup Time: ~4–6 weeks | Best For: Multi-investor syndicate & institutional governance.
OPTION B Direct Title

Direct 100% Foreign Owned LLC (FIE)

Foreign investors directly appear as the named equity owners on the Vietnamese Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) and Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC).

  • ✓ Direct Legal Title: No intermediaries; 100% direct ownership of machinery, bank accounts & leases in Vietnam.
  • ✓ Direct Bank Remittance: After-tax profit wired directly from DICA bank account to investor personal accounts (5% PIT on individual dividends).
  • ✓ Zero Offshore Costs: No ongoing offshore corporate maintenance or registered agent fees.
Setup Time: ~4–7 weeks | Best For: Single primary investor or close-knit partners wanting direct on-shore title.
OPTION C Fastest Launch

Local Nominee Company + Trust / Loan Agreement

A trusted local Vietnamese founding partner sets up a standard domestic LLC (1-day setup) backed by a legally binding Trust Deed, Power of Attorney, and Convertible Loan Contract.

  • ✓ Lightning Fast Execution: Instant bank accounts & permits within 3–5 days (start renovation immediately).
  • ✓ Secured Legal Backing: Foreign investor holds mortgage over assets, irrevocable voting proxy, and call-option to convert to 100% FIE at any time.
  • ✓ Flexible Profit Extraction: Profit distributions structured via service contracts, royalty licensing, or quarterly dividend mandates.
Setup Time: ~3–5 days | Best For: Fast lease securing & proof-of-concept phase before converting to Option A.

Step-by-Step Execution Playbook

Implementation Playbook: Who Does What & Bank Setup

Detailed sequence of corporate incorporation, bank account opening (DICA vs. Current), capital injection, and store handover.

Option A Implementation Flow: Singapore Holding SPV → Vietnam LLC

Institutional corporate structure with Singapore holding vehicle and Vietnam operating subsidiary.

TIMELINE: ~4–6 WEEKS EST. SETUP COST: ~$3,500 – $4,800 USD
Stage 1: Incorporate Singapore SPV & Corporate Bank Account (Days 1–5) Cost: ~$1,000 – $1,500 SGD (~$800–$1,100 USD)

• Who does what: Foreign Investors + Singapore Corporate Secretary (e.g. Osome / Sleek).
• Actions: ACRA filing fee (S$315), 1-year registered address & local company secretary, drafting Shareholders' Agreement (SHA).
• Bank Setup: Open Singapore multi-currency corporate account with DBS, OCBC, or Aspire (Free / ~$0 setup fee; ~$20/mo maintenance).

Stage 2: Notarize SG Documents & Vietnam IRC/ERC Filing (Weeks 2–4) Cost: ~$2,200 – $3,000 USD

• Who does what: SG Notary Public & Vietnam Licensed Legal Counsel (e.g. Baker Tilly / Acclime / Local Law Firm).
• Legal Fees: ~$1,800 – $2,500 USD for complete IRC (Investment Registration Certificate) + ERC (Enterprise Registration Certificate) filing package.
• Notary & Consular Legalisation: ~$400 – $600 USD (Apostille/Embassy seal of SG incorporation documents and charter).
• Gov Licensing Fees: ~500,000 – 1,000,000 VNĐ (~$20–$40 USD) official state registry fee.

Stage 3: Vietnam DICA Bank Accounts & Capital Injection (Weeks 5–6) Cost: ~$300 – $500 USD

• Who does what: Vietnam General Director & Custodian Commercial Bank (HSBC / Standard Chartered / Vietcombank).
• Bank Setup Fees: HSBC initial deposit (~$300 USD) + token keys / swift fees; account maintenance ~$15–$25 USD/mo.
• Account Structure: (1) Direct Investment Capital Account (DICA) in USD/VND (receives equity injection), and (2) Operating Current Account in VND.
• Digital Token & Corporate Seal: 2,500,000 VNĐ (~$100 USD) for official company seal & tax e-token.

Option B Implementation Flow: Direct Foreign Individual/Corporate LLC

Direct on-shore registration with foreign individuals/companies named directly on Vietnam license.

TIMELINE: ~4–7 WEEKS EST. SETUP COST: ~$2,500 – $3,500 USD
Stage 1: Passport & Financial Proof Legalisation (Weeks 1–2) Cost: ~$300 – $600 USD

• Who does what: Foreign Investors + Vietnamese Embassy / Home Country Notary.
• Notary & Embassy Legalisation: Notarizing and certifying investor passport copies + official bank balance confirmation letters (proving investment capital availability).

Stage 2: DPI Licensing in Ho Chi Minh City (Weeks 3–5) Cost: ~$1,800 – $2,500 USD

• Who does what: Vietnam Legal Counsel files directly with DPI HCMC.
• Legal Fees: ~$1,800 – $2,200 USD for drafting feasibility study, company charter, IRC, and ERC applications.
• Gov Licensing Fees: ~1,000,000 VNĐ (~$40 USD) statutory registration charges.

Stage 3: DICA Account & Direct Wire (Weeks 6–7) Cost: ~$300 – $400 USD

• Who does what: Investor & Custodian Bank (Vietcombank / HSBC).
• Bank Setup: Opening DICA (Direct Investment Capital Account) + Operating VND account. Bank token, company seal registration (~$100 USD).
• Wire Capital: Investors wire funds directly from personal overseas accounts into the DICA account.

Option C Implementation Flow: Domestic Setup + Trust / Convertible Loan (Fast-Track)

Fastest execution route allowing immediate lease signing and construction within 5 days.

TIMELINE: ~3–5 DAYS EST. SETUP COST: ~$1,000 – $1,800 USD
Stage 1: Domestic LLC Registration & Corporate Bank Account (Days 1–3) Cost: ~$300 – $500 USD (7M–12M VNĐ)

• Who does what: Local Vietnamese Managing Partner / Operator.
• Cost Breakdown: Domestic business license registration (2M VNĐ), corporate seal + e-invoice token (3M VNĐ), initial state business licensing tax (2M VNĐ).
• Bank Setup: Corporate payment account opened in 24 hours at Techcombank / ACB / Vietcombank ($0 setup fee).

Stage 2: Sign Trust Deed, Convertible Loan & Mortgage Pledge (Days 3–4) Cost: ~$600 – $1,200 USD

• Who does what: Foreign Investors & Local Founding Partner + Notary Law Office.
• Legal Drafting Fees: ~$600 – $1,000 USD to draft tripartite security contracts: (1) Irrevocable Power of Attorney, (2) Secured Convertible Loan Agreement, (3) Asset mortgage pledge over all machinery, and (4) Call Option conversion clause.
• Notarization: ~$100 – $200 USD local public notary fee.

Stage 3: Immediate Lease Signing & Construction Kickoff (Day 5) Cost: $0 Added Legal Cost

• Who does what: Store management team executes lease agreement with Japan Town landlord directly under the domestic LLC.
• Outcome: Save 5–6 weeks of idle waiting time and avoid paying holding rent while licensing is processed.

Standard Operating Procedure

Annual Profit Distribution & Remittance Flow

📑

Step 1: Annual Statutory Audit

Annual financial audit conducted by an independent certified audit firm (such as Baker Tilly, Mazars, or Crowe) alongside the Corporate Income Tax (CIT) annual finalization.

🏛️

Step 2: 7-Day Tax Authority Notice

Submit formal notification of profit remittance to the HCMC Tax Department at least 7 working days prior to the transfer (Circular No. 186/2010/TT-BTC compliance).

💳

Step 3: Direct Bank Wire Remittance

The custodian commercial bank (HSBC/Vietcombank) processes the wire transfer from the capital account directly to investor offshore accounts in USD, SGD, or JPY.
