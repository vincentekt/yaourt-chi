Strategic Moat

Yuki-Chi's 4 Value Drivers

⚡

1. High Table & Space Velocity

The weigh-and-pay model eliminates kitchen bottlenecks. Average customer journey is under 3 minutes from entrance to payment, driving 3x higher throughput than sit-down Bingsu cafes.

🥑

2. Health & Guilt-Free Positioning

Captures the surging wellness trend in Vietnam with genuine unsweetened Brazilian Acai and live probiotic yogurt, replacing sugar-laden traditional desserts.

📸

3. Viral Creator Economy Flywheel

The visual contrast of deep purple acai, green matcha, colorful mochi cubes, and golden warm sauce pours creates effortless organic UGC on TikTok and Instagram.
